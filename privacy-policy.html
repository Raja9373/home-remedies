Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 26, 2026 — How HomeRemediesWorld.com handles visitor privacy, cookies, local storage, and Google AdSense advertising.

1. Introduction & Data Minimization

At HomeRemediesWorld.com, your privacy is respected and protected. We operate as an educational archive of global home remedy traditions, acupressure points, and yoga hast mudras. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect or sell personally identifiable information (PII) on our own servers.

Any interactive features such as saved questions in Dadi Se Poocho or draft notes in Dadi Ka Nuskha Bhejo are stored locally inside your own web browser (localStorage) and stay strictly on your device unless you choose to clear your browser data.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

HomeRemediesWorld.com uses Google AdSense to display advertisements and keep this informational resource free for everyone worldwide.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick / DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to our visitors based on their visit to HomeRemediesWorld.com and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings (My Ad Center) or by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.
  • For more details on how Google uses information from sites that use its services, please review Google's Privacy & Terms for Partner Sites.

3. Log Files & Technical Information

Like most standard websites, our hosting infrastructure may record non-personally-identifying technical log information such as browser type, device type, referring pages, approximate country/region, and timestamps for security, uptime, and performance monitoring. These logs are never linked to your personal identity.

4. GDPR, CCPA & Global User Rights

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, California, or other regions with data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Control or disable cookies directly through your browser settings or Google Ad Settings.
  • Clear local browser storage (localStorage) at any time from your browser privacy controls.
  • Contact us with any privacy request or concern regarding information displayed on the site.

5. Children's Privacy

HomeRemediesWorld.com does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable age of digital consent). If you believe any content or message submitted via email involves a minor's personal data, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

6. Contact Us About Privacy

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, cookie choices, or site content, please write to us at info@homeremediesworld.com or visit our Contact Us page.
